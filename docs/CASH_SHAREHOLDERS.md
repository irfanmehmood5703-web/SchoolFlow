# Cash Report and Shareholders reference batch

Cash Report uses the supplied date, multi-campus, multi-user and hierarchical multi-account filters. Account class/group checkboxes select descendants; search, select-all and clear-selection controls are included. No account selection means all configured accounts, including inactive accounts with historical activity. Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Expense and Income tabs show grouped historical account snapshots. Summary rows contain group/class rollups and a single grand total calculated from account rows, avoiding double counting. Brought-forward columns contain gross posted debit/credit before From; duration columns cover the inclusive selected dates; balance is brought debit minus credit plus duration debit minus credit. This uses actual SchoolFlow journal history rather than copying the reference's example zero openings.

Section search/paging never changes summary totals. Complete-report print ignores section search/paging and includes all loaded account classes, the date/campus/user/account scope and school branding. No new financial postings are made by this report.

Shareholders implements the supplied directory/dashboard and Add Shareholder fields: name, equity percentage, optional linked Equity account, whole-institute or campus scope, CNIC, contact, email, Partner Since, active partner and notes. Save/Cancel, edit, former-partner toggle, date range and print are available. Linked posted Equity activity supplies cumulative capital as at To, period credit/debit amounts and transaction counts. Unlinked capital is explicitly unknown, never fabricated as zero. A read-only linked-account transaction view reuses actual journal vouchers; it does not create contributions, withdrawals or payouts.

Profit estimates use current percentage times scoped journal profit, from the later of From or Partner Since, rounded to integer paisa. Former partners show no current allocation estimate. These semantics are labelled in the UI: the supplied references do not establish historical ownership-effective-date/proration or distribution rules. Active ownership cannot exceed 100% in any campus, including overlapping whole-institute ownership. Overlapping partners cannot share the same capital account, preventing duplicated capital. Linked accounts cannot be deleted, and posted capital links cannot be replaced. Stale edits, invalid scopes/accounts/percentages and changed-payload token retries are rejected. Exact new-partner retries are actor-bound and do not duplicate partners. Changes are audited under the institute write lock and CSRF protection.
