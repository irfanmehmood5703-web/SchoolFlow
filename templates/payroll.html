Set working calendar for {{month}}

Monthly basic salary stays fixed for full months of any length. In the all-calendar-days policy (paid Sundays and holidays), daily percentage deductions and bonus use a fixed 30-day salary rate. Joining-month basic pay is prorated by actual eligible paid dates; working-dates-only plans retain their working-day calculation. Attendance is assessed only on the saved working dates. Personal leave deductions follow the approved salary plan. Approved manual increments apply only to employees whose salary plan enables them. Saved monthly attendance totals take precedence over daily counts for deductions and lecture incentives; changed daily records or calendars require review. All-present bonuses still require complete dated attendance.
