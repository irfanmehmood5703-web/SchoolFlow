Formula to review

The due date itself has no fine. Automation/assessment waits until the grace window has passed. Once eligible, per-day amounts count all calendar days after the saved due date, including the grace days. Fixed amounts apply once after grace. The maximum caps the lifetime fine on one challan.

Example: due 10th + 2 grace days → first eligible date is 13th; per-day assessment then counts 3 overdue days. Saving a policy never charges a bill. Only fully unpaid, undiscounted, unfrozen challans of active students are supported here. No fine is calculated on another fine.

On the first applied assessment, the policy, label and due-date basis are saved on the challan. Later assessments retain that saved rule and add only the outstanding difference. Current policy edits apply to previously unassessed challans, using the student’s current campus. The preview shows that cohort; historical billing cohorts are not inferred.
