Salary plan calculation rules

Joining-month basic pay uses the saved salary pay basis in Payroll Working Calendar, which can include paid Sundays and school holidays. Manual increments are optional: enter an employee, amount and effective month under Increment, approve it, then enable manual increments in their plan. Basic Salary is the amount before those approved increments. Sandwich leave remains disabled. Approved manual increments are available; automatic annual increments remain off. Grouping and maximum rules below use the explicitly selected SchoolFlow calculation mode. The all-present bonus pays one daily salary rate of basic plus bonusable earnings; every eligible working day must be marked Present or Off Day, with at least one Present. A disqualifying attendance status cancels it. Missing days receive no bonus.

Attendance Based Deductions

Set each attendance status independently. Allowed days (no deduction) are the number of days per salary month that are free from that status's deduction.

Example: If Absent allowed = 0 and Leave allowed = 2, the first two days marked Leave have no leave deduction, while days marked Absent are still deducted according to the Absent rule. A third Leave day is charged only at the configured Leave deduction rate. Late has its own separate allowance and rate. Unmarked days are not counted.

These rules are included when you save the salary plan above or below.

Allowed days are subtracted separately from the recorded count of each attendance status; unused leave allowance never offsets Absent or Late. Every extra day uses group size 0. For groups of 2, five extra days produce 2 complete groups or 3 started groups. Once limits the result to one event. An events cap limits events; a PKR cap limits the rounded money amount. Zero with an explicit cap means no deduction; No cap ignores maximum 0. For paid personal leave, set Allowed days (for example, 2) or a deduction of 0 for unlimited paid leave. To deduct unpaid leave after its allowance, enter a fixed amount or a percentage of the daily basic salary. Percentage rules divide monthly basic by the saved salary-paid day count, including paid Sundays and holidays if selected. These are explicit SchoolFlow rules; choose the interpretation used by your school before approving the plan.
