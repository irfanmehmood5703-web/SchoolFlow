School-data testing order

  1. Sign in to a private institute and configure its name, campuses, session, classes and sections under Institute Setup.
  2. Add one student and family. Sign out and sign in again; confirm that the same saved profile and photograph remain.
  3. Set fee heads and a student fee plan; check discounts, create one challan, record one payment and verify its reports. Use the explicit reversal flow for a correction.
  4. Mark attendance, create a date sheet and award list, save marks, then check result and roll-slip outputs.
  5. Add one employee, configure the working calendar and salary plan, approve and calculate a completed month, then verify pay/unpay and reports.
  6. Review Finance, General, certificates, Parent/Teacher access, Messages, Support and the built-in Guide one option at a time.

Approve each option against the school’s own figures before entering a larger batch. No audit, test or initial page visit sends a message, pays a salary or posts a journal automatically.

Implemented web workflows to verify with your school

  • Institute Code, owner/staff web login, campus permissions, payment approvals, audit logs and institute data isolation.
  • Student and employee forms, dropdown settings, documents, family linking, reviewed import and guarded batch undo.
  • Institute, Exam and HRM settings; campus/class maps, subjects, shifts, roll series, certificates and branding.
  • Fee heads and charging timings, scoped fee schedules, student fee plans, discounts, net-fee increments and challan generation.
  • Fee payments, reversals, installments, history, defaulter reports and fee audit with historical billing cohorts.
  • Student Fee Sheet provides a twelve-month student grid with Paid/Payable/Discount/Remaining choices, CSV/Excel and print. Lecture Delivery provides dated register filters, approvals, attendance import links and saved-salary linkage.
  • Attendance, assessment marks/results, date sheets, hall assignments, roll slips and teacher-performance reports.
  • Payroll plans, working-day joining proration, manual approved increments, salary generation/pay/unpay and lecture registers/rates.
  • Balanced journal, income and expense posting, chart of accounts, trial balance, profit/loss, General Ledger, cash and shareholder reports.
  • Requisition approval, staff advances, split bills and attachments, returns, carry-forward, reimbursement and ageing reconciliation.
  • Daily Snapshots record observed fee, attendance and enrolment history. Posted ledger income/expense now refresh after Finance saves; previous unknown captures remain unavailable.
  • Private participant Messages, human Support tickets, personal notification bell and built-in free SchoolFlow Guide.
  • Web QR/gate and geo-attendance endpoints and permission checks are implemented; physical devices and connected mobile apps require separate verification.

Partial or incomplete workflows

  • Automatic fee receipt posting is available under Ledger Setup → Automatic Fee Ledger Posting. Enable it only after mapping accounts. New receipts post cash-basis gross income and discounts atomically; reversals post on the current date. Earlier receipts are not backfilled. Payroll-to-General-Ledger automatic posting remains separate work.
  • Report/filter/print parity and remaining template tag values still need reference-specific checks. All 37 reference widgets are implemented under General → Widgets, with source access checks and explicit unavailable telemetry.
  • Manual employee increments are implemented; recurring annual increments and sandwich leave are disabled as requested. A saved Consecutive lecture preference does not create future lectures; the school enters and approves delivered lectures explicitly. Fee and payroll calculations follow their displayed saved rules, not guessed reference rules.
  • Daily history cannot backfill unobserved dates. Old saved snapshots remain frozen, including after backdated journals; current captures refresh enabled history.

External connections and launch work

  • Four Android test packages are published for Teacher, Parent, Owner and the school-SIM SMS sender. Teacher/Parent/Owner use the existing web portal and private account permissions. The attended SMS client connects a school SIM to the reviewed outbox; no push notifications or offline sync are included.
  • WhatsApp/branded SMS delivery requires a working sender/provider; saving a request does not send it. WhatsApp setup controls do not by themselves establish provider delivery.
  • Registered bank consumer numbers and automatic bank collection are not connected.
  • Free sleeping hosts cannot promise uninterrupted scheduled reminders, fine jobs, attendance jobs or snapshot capture. Configure and verify the required schedule before relying on automatic runs.
  • General → App Links supplies the Gate School Test APK for this original host. Register the school gate before attended enrollment/marking and verify it on the actual phone. The separate Gate Trial APK stays on its fictional trial server. Physical recognition accuracy and production signing are not certified by emulator tests.
  • Physical Android/device testing, production hardening and tested backup/restore procedures remain launch requirements.
