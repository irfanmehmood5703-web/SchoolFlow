How this page works

Critical: access, approval, reversal or deletion. Important: student, employee, exam or financial changes. Normal: settings. Routine: recorded attendance or queued-message work, hidden unless selected.

Details compare only saved before/after values. Legacy entries may have no recorded actor, record link or field snapshot. “System” is used only for a known background/operator context. Passwords, secrets and tokens are hidden. This page does not change or delete logs, send daily summaries or silently archive history.
