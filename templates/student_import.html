School Migration Center

Move an existing school into SchoolFlow without re-entering every student. Complete the setup first, then download, upload, review and confirm each file.

Recommended order: Configure campus, classes, session, fee heads and fee plans → download student template → fill one row per student → upload and confirm → download old receipts template → fill and upload → review migration totals → verify students and dues in the live modules.

Opening dues: Enter only unpaid amounts in Opening arrears — Fee Head. Example: old tuition Rs. 3,500, paid Rs. 1,500, outstanding Rs. 2,000 → enter Rs. 2,000. Old payments are retained as reference records, not new cash collections.

Safety: Review errors before confirmation. Keep original old-system receipts and balance reports. The control report is not an automatic General Ledger reconciliation.
